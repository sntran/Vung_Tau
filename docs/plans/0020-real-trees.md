# 0020 Real trees

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Built |

## 1. Goal

The trees of Vũng Tàu look real. A player who knows the town sees the coconut palms of Bãi Trước, the casuarinas of Bãi Sau, the bàng trees of the park, and the forest on Núi Lớn and Núi Nhỏ, and knows them. The trees move in the wind, cast shadows, and change with the season of the game date.

The box shapes of plan 0005 (`TreeKit`) go away.

## 2. Out of scope

- The trees on the far hills at every distance. Defect D-0005-01 of plan 0005 adds them first, with simple far forms. This plan replaces the near and middle forms, and the far form if it is better.
- Garden plants and fruit trees of the homestay (plan 0022).
- Tết plants: the pots of hoa mai and the other plants of the flower market (plan 0021).
- Grass, bushes, and flowers on the ground.
- Trees that fall, are cut, or grow.

## 3. Decisions

| ID | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The trees are made by code in Blender (`bpy` from PyPI, the version of plan 0006), from the values in `data/trees/types.json`. The script exports a `.glb` for each type and variant. | We have no artist. Free libraries have few tropical trees, and the workspace cannot download from `dl.polyhaven.org`. Code lets me change a tree with numbers. Plan 0006 already runs Blender in the workspace and in CI. The prototype `docs/prototype/trees/trees.py` makes a coconut palm and a bàng tree in about 40 s each. |
| D2 | The prototype pictures `docs/prototype/trees/trees_v3.jpg` show the target look. | I judge a look from a picture, not from text. |
| D3 | Each type has three forms: near (real leaves and leaflets), middle (leaf cards: flat pictures of leaf groups with alpha), and far (an impostor: a picture of the tree from many directions, on a camera-facing card). Godot changes the form by distance with `visibility_range_begin`, `visibility_range_end`, and a fade. | A tree with real leaves has tens of thousands of triangles. The town has about 29 000 street trees and many more hill trees. Only the near trees can have real leaves. |
| D4 | The leaves use alpha scissor, not alpha blend. | Blended leaves sort wrong and cannot cast shadows. Scissored leaves can. |
| D5 | Each type has at least 3 variants with different seeds. The world adds a random turn and size to each tree. | Copies of one tree in a row look false. |
| D6 | Wind moves the trees in the vertex shader: the trunk a little, the branches more, the palm fronds and the leaves most. The wind of the game sets the strength and the direction. | Trees that do not move look dead. A shader costs almost nothing. |
| D7 | Only facts with the status "Source" or "Confirmed" in `docs/content/culture-notes.md` choose the tree types of a place. | I know the town. A guess must not become a fact. |
| D8 | Where trees stand on the hills, and how tall they are, comes from public data: the Meta and WRI canopy height maps (about 1 m) and ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m classes), both CC BY 4.0 (added 2026-10-26, report 0005, D-0005-09). | The game must not guess the forest. The data shows the real gaps, the dense parts, and the bare granite. |

## 4. Requirements

### The tree types

- **R1.** Types and their first values (the builder sets the sizes from sources and adds them to `data/trees/types.json` with the status "check"):

| Type | Where | Look |
|---|---|---|

A round crown of 22 to 26 fronds of 5 to 6 m that arch out, with tips that curve down. The leaflets hang down from each frond like a curtain. Dead brown fronds hang under the crown. Green and brown coconuts. |

In the dry season, some leaves are red and orange, and some fall (Q4). |

- **R2.** `data/trees/types.json` holds the values of each type: the sizes, the counts of branches, fronds, and leaves, the colors of each season, the flower season, and the seeds of the variants. Code does not hold these values.
- **R3.** The build script (`tools/trees/build.py`) makes the forms of R5 for each type and variant: `assets/trees/TYPE_VARIANT.glb` and the impostor textures. It is in CI and in the workspace. It is deterministic: the same values give the same files.

### The look

- **R4.** At the near distance, each type looks like the target picture for its type (D2), under the light of the game. The leaves let light through when the sun is behind them.
- **R5.** The forms and the distances of each preset:

| Preset | Near form to | Middle form to | Far form to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 20 m | 120 m | 900 m |
| Medium | 35 m | 200 m | 900 m |
| High | 60 m | 300 m | 1 500 m |
| Ultra | 90 m | 400 m | 2 000 m |

- **R6.** A change of form is not visible as a jump: the forms fade, and the mean color of the middle and far forms is within 8 % of the near form, under the same light.
- **R7.** The trees cast shadows inside the shadow distance of the preset. The far form does not cast shadows.
- **R8.** Wind (D6). In calm air, the leaves still move a little.
- **R9.** The season (`scripts/season.gd`) changes the colors, the share of bare branches, and the flowers of each type, as `data/trees/types.json` says.

### In the world

- **R10.** `StreetTrees` and the hill trees of `scenes/world/vung_tau.gd` use the new types. The mixes in `data/geo/trees.json` use the new type names. Bãi Trước has coconut palms along the shore and in the park (culture notes).
- **R10a.** The hill trees stand where the canopy height map has trees. The height of each tree comes from the map (the type sets the shape, the map sets the size), and the crowns are as wide as the canopy patches. Where WorldCover says shrubland, the hills have `scrub`. Where it says grassland or bare, the ground shows. The street and park trees keep the rules of `data/geo/trees.json`, but a street tree that the canopy map does not show (a gap of 5 m or more) is checked in the report.
- **R11.** No tree stands on the sand of Bãi Trước or in the water. The boats are in the bay, as plan 0005 (Q6) says.
- **R12.** Budget (plan 0004, R14): at the views of PT-54, Low stays at 600 draw calls and 300 000 triangles or less, and High at 2 000 draw calls and 2 000 000 triangles or less.

## 8. Builder notes

- **N1. The build** (`tools/trees/build.py`, D1, R2, R3). Blender 5.2.3 as a Python module (`tools/trees/requirements.txt`) makes the trees from `data/trees/types.json`. A generator for each kind of tree (palm, fan palm, bàng and the broadleaf trees, frangipani, casuarina, flame tree, Norfolk pine, scrub) makes the wood (tubes on a path) and leaf groups. A leaf group is a template (a spray of leaf rosettes, one side of a palm frond part, a casuarina tuft, a fern-like flame tree leaf group, a fan leaf, a pine branchlet) in a frame. The near form puts the real leaves of the template in each frame. The middle form puts a card in each frame (every k-th group, a little larger, when there are more than `max_cards`), with a picture of the template from above (`TYPE_cards.png`), so the two forms have the same leaves in the same places. The far form is a picture of the near form of each variant from 8 directions (`TYPE_impostor.png` and `TYPE_impostor_normal.png`). Cycles renders the pictures with one thread, so the same values give the same files. `build.py --check` (the "Tree build check" job of CI) makes all files again and compares them: the meshes must be the same, and at most 1 % of the pixels of a picture can be different (another processor). `assets/trees/manifest.json` has the triangles, the sizes, and the hashes.
- **N2. The data in the meshes.** The vertex color holds data, not a color: R is a random value of the leaf, G is how much the wind moves the point (0 at the trunk base, 1 at the tips), B is the kind (0 wood, 0.5 leaf, 1 dead leaf or brown coconut), and A is the shade inside the crown. The pictures hold the shade, the random value, the kind, and the cover. The normals of the leaves and the cards point half out of the crown (D3, R6), so all forms get almost the same light. I checked in the workspace that Godot reads the vertex colors and the custom normals of a glTF file as they are.
- **N3. The game** (D3 to D6, R5 to R9). `TreeLibrary` (`scripts/trees/tree_library.gd`) loads the meshes and makes the materials of each type: the leaves (`tree_leaf.gdshader`), the cards (`tree_card.gdshader`, alpha scissor, with more alpha in the small mipmaps so that far cards keep their cover), the wood (`tree_wood.gdshader`, bark stripes, palm rings), and the impostor (`tree_impostor.gdshader`: a card that turns to the camera and shows the two nearest directions). `tree_common.gdshaderinc` has the wind, the season colors, and the change of form: each tree fades from one form to the next with a dither by its distance from the view point, with the same dither for both forms, in a band of 20 % of the range (3 m to 60 m). The ranges of R5 and R13 are the global shader value `tree_ranges`, which `Quality.apply()` sets. The scrub has 45 % of the ranges and no far form (the ground shader draws the bushes far away). The hill types have 70 % of the ranges: in the dense forest of Núi Nhỏ, the near form to 60 m had 121 trees on High, more than the budget (R12).
- **N4. The tree field** (`scenes/world/tree_field.gd`). All trees are in cells of 32 m, in groups that come and go together: the street and park trees, and the trees of each near chunk. When the view moves 4 m, the field puts the trees near it in one MultiMesh for each type, variant, and form. The middle trees farther than the shadow distance of the preset are in a list with no shadow, and the shadow of a near tree comes from its middle form (R7, R12). The street and park trees have their far form in fixed tiles of 512 m. The far form of the hill trees is in the lists near the view out to the end of the near chunks (300 m), and the clumps of `HillCanopy` stay beyond.
- **N5. The wind** (`scripts/wind.gd`, D6, R8). The global shader values `wind_direction` and `wind_strength` come from `data/trees/types.json` ("wind": from the northeast, stronger in the afternoon). The trunk bends a little, the tips more, and the leaves flutter, also in calm air. `Wind.hold()` keeps a wind for PT-55.
- **N6. The places** (R10, R11). `data/geo/trees.json` uses the new types: Quang Trung (the coast road of Bãi Trước) has bàng and coconut palms, the parks near Bãi Trước mostly coconut palms with some bàng and a few casuarinas, Trần Hưng Đạo has `tall_shade`, and Ba Cu has casuarinas and flame trees (`named_roads`, a new part of `tools/geo/geodata/trees.py`). The other main roads have shade trees, bàng, flame trees, and frangipanis until Q1 has an answer. The Norfolk pine row of Quang Trung is not in the game until Q6 has an answer. PT-53 finds no tree on the sand of the main beach and none in the water.
- **N7. The measured values.** Near form: 1 760 (scrub) to 18 700 (flame tree) triangles; middle form: 130 to 680. PT-54 under lavapipe at 1 280 × 720, the part of the trees (the view with the trees minus the view without them): Low 113 to 125 draw calls and 104 000 to 122 000 triangles (half the limit: 300 and 150 000); High 144 to 161 draw calls and 531 000 to 837 000 triangles (half the limit: 1 000 and 1 000 000). The whole view was more than R12 on Low because of the ground (Q7). After the detail levels of the ground (report 0005, D-0005-10), PT-54 checks the whole view against R12 again: Low 226 000 to 275 000 triangles, High 1 080 000 to 1 287 000 (plan 0005, N40).
- **N8. The play tests.** PT-52 runs in two scenarios (Q8), each in phases of a group of types: a phase takes less than the time limit under lavapipe. The CI has 8 parts now. The R6 check compares the same view (the same camera and light) with the form before the end of a form and with the form after it: for each picture, the test moves the ranges for a moment. With two camera positions, short trees changed their look by the view, not by the form. The check needed more cover in the far form of the thin types (`impostor_cover`: casuarina, Norfolk pine, `hill_c`), other card sizes (`card_scale`: larger for the Norfolk pine and the hill types, smaller for the casuarina and the scrub), and more cards for the hill types.
- **N9. The trees from the canopy map** (D8, R10a). `build.py` puts the trees of the open land (not the built-up and the cropland classes of WorldCover) on the canopy height map: the highest canopy first, then the lower canopy, with a tree on each cell of 2 m that is not nearer to a placed tree than its spacing (0.9 of its height, 5 m to 12 m). The map has only about one height top for each 300 m² of the forest (a group of crowns), so a tree on each top gave crowns of 8 m in radius on trees of 10 m. The crown of a tree is the canopy that is nearer to it than to another tree; its radius is that of a circle with the same area. `canopy_trees.json` has 92 454 trees (mean height 6.8 m, mean crown radius 4.4 m): 16 334 on Núi Lớn and 7 251 on Núi Nhỏ. The game stands a hill tree on each one in the near chunks: the height comes from the map (the scale is the map height over the height of the variant, from 0.5 to 2.0), and the type is the one whose crown radius over height is nearest to that of the crown (with a small weight for the height). In the area of Núi Lớn, 15 % of the trees of 10 m or more are teak (`data/trees/types.json`, "hills"). The scrub stands where `canopy.png` and the cover map have a bush (0.6 m to 3 m in "tree cover", with that height) and, less dense, under the trees; not on grassland or bare ground, also with a low canopy there (PT-33 checks it near Bãi Sau). WorldCover has almost no shrubland here, so the bushes come from the canopy heights. The canopy map shows no tree within 5 m of 14 600 of the 29 060 street and park trees (the report lists the share of each type): they stay, and I check them on photos. PT-54 on High, the part of the trees: 504 000 to 850 000 triangles (half the limit: 1 000 000).
- **N10. The places with no trees (report 0020, D-0020-01).** `data/geo/clearings.json` lists them: the OSM areas with `man_made`, `tourism`, `amenity`, `historic`, or `building` (30 000 m² or less), the pedestrian areas (`highway` with `area=yes`, for example the yard of the lighthouse), and a circle around the landmarks of the hill tops (Núi Nhỏ 25 m, the lighthouse 20 m, Tượng Chúa Kitô Vua 25 m, Núi Lớn 15 m, Tượng Đức Mẹ Bãi Dâu 12 m, Miếu Hòn Bà 6 m), each with a margin of 2 m. There, `build.py` takes the trees and the bushes out of `cover.png` and puts no tree in `canopy_trees.json`, so the scrub, the far clumps, and the ground canopy are not there either. No tree trunk stands on a road or a path (the road width and 1.5 m). The report gives the area (273 ha, most of it buildings of the town). PT-33 draws each view again with the far plane of the camera at 3 m and two background colors: the pixels that stay the same show things nearer than 3 m, and they must be 5 % of the frame or less. With the data before, the view from Núi Nhỏ along Bãi Sau had 17.7 %; now each view has 0 %.
- **N11. The street trees and the canopy map (report 0020, D-0020-03).** `build.py` finds the tree tops of the canopy map on all the land (also in the town, not on the places of N10 or on the roads): 142 000 tops. On the roads of my photos (Quang Trung, Ba Cu, Trần Hưng Đạo, Thùy Vân, Hạ Long, and Trần Phú) and in the park of Bãi Trước, the rows of `data/geo/trees.json` stay, and each tree moves to the nearest free tree top within 6 m (8 % to 23 % of them). The coconut palms there stay also where the map has no tree. On the other main roads, a tree stands only on a tree top within 6 m of the road edge (out of the road surface: a crown over the road has its trunk at the edge), with the mix of the road; in the other parks, only on the tree tops in the park. The street and park trees are now 12 550 (they were 29 060). The report gives a table for each road group: the trees, the part that moved to a tree top, and the part with no canopy within 5 m (72 % on the coast roads and on Quang Trung: the map misses most palms and casuarinas; 0 % on the other roads). `canopy` in `data/geo/trees.json` has the distances.
- **N12. The leaves and the looks (report 0020, D-0020-02 and D-0020-04).** `hill_a`, `hill_b`, `hill_c`, and `shade` have a new leaf group (`leaf_cluster` in `data/trees/types.json`, `template_cluster` in the build): a spray with 12 short twigs of 10 small leaves each, 11 cm to 12 cm long. A small leaf is 1 triangle, so that the crown can be a mass of leaves with gaps for the triangles it has; the trees also have 3 sprays on each branch (2 before). Their near form has 15 000 to 25 000 triangles (5 000 before; `shade` 12 000). So their near form ends at 0.6 of its range (`near_scale`, a new value of a type: for example 25 m on High for the hill types), and the cards of the middle form, with the same small leaves, show farther. `teak` and `bang` keep their large leaves. The scrub has the same kind of spray (8 twigs of 7 leaves of 9 cm) and 56 sprays. The coconut palm has darker green fronds (`#3F5E21`, young `#5E7A2B`). The fan palm has 14 round fan leaves on stems of 2 m, from 30° to 110° from the vertical, which face out along their stems (it was 22 leaves on stems of 1.3 m, a round mass). The Norfolk pine waits (Q6). For the R6 check on Low and High, the far form of `hill_a`, `hill_b`, and `hill_c` has more cover (`impostor_cover` 1.3), the cards of `hill_a` are smaller (`card_scale` 1.15, was 1.25) and those of `hill_c` too (1.0, was 1.15: on High its middle form was 12 % darker than the near form), and the scrub has smaller cards (`card_scale` 0.62, was 0.85). PT-52 does not check the leaf share of a type with no far form (the scrub) farther than its middle range: the ground draws the bushes there. PT-54 after these changes: Low 193 000 to 264 000 triangles, High 972 000 to 1 134 000.
