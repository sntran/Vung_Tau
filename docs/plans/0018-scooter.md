## 1. Goal

The player can get on a scooter that is parked in the street, ride it through the town, and get off anywhere. The game clock runs at the speed of real time, so a slow ride along the coast at sunset takes the time that it took in real life. On a touch screen, the player sees the controls.

The scooter is first a tool to move fast: for me and for an agent that tests the town. The rules of real life come in later parts (section 2).

## 2. Out of scope

These come in later parts of this plan, each with its own approval:

- The player rents the scooter of the homestay from cô Nga, and rides only that scooter (it needs the homestay of plan 0009, R11).
- The helmet, and the reactions of people to a rider with no helmet.
- Paid parking (gửi xe) with a ticket at the market and at the beach.
- Fuel, a horn, and a passenger.
- Traffic: other scooters, cars, and buses that move on the streets (later in this plan, with lanes and junctions).
- A model of a 50 cm³ moped from World. Until World has one, the parked scooter of World (`scooter_tay_ga_1`) rides with the limits of a 50 cm³ moped (D2).

## 4. Requirements

### The clock

- **R1.** `GameState` moves the clock by the time of the frames (D6), with the seconds, and saves the seconds. `add_walk()` no longer moves the clock (`walked_total_m` stays for the play tests). The clock does not move while the game waits for a load of the world at its start. Unit tests: 60 s of frames give 1 minute; a walk of 100 m gives no minute; an action with a time cost, a wait of the Clock app, and the sleep still move the clock.
- **R2.** Each play test that counts on the rule of 8 m for each minute changes to the new rule. `PlaytestDriver.set_time_scale()` speeds up the clock and the world together, as now.

### The touch controls

- **R3.** The joystick at its rest place, and the "Get off" button (D7). They do not cover the phone, the subtitles, or the center dot, at 1280 × 720, 2400 × 1080, and 720 × 1280.

### The ride

- **R4.** Each parked scooter of World in the game can be ridden: the rows of `data/world/parked_rows.json`, the scooters of the test street, and the rows of the streets of plan 0009 when they come. When the player looks at one within 2 m, the center dot shows the action "Ride" (Vietnamese and English text in `data/`). The scooter leaves its row while the player rides it, and goes back into a row of its own where the player gets off.
- **R5.** The motion of D2, D4, and D5. A unit test checks the side acceleration of the full turn at 5, 10, 20, 30, and 40 km/h (at most 3.5 m/s²). The wheels turn with the speed, the front wheel and the handlebars turn with the steering (the joints of World), and the stand goes up. The scooter follows the ground: its pitch from the ground under its two wheels.
- **R6.** The view of D3.
- **R7.** The sound of the engine: a loop whose pitch follows the speed, the start, and the bump, with captions in `data/sounds.json` ("[xe máy nổ máy]", "[xe máy chạy]", "[cộp]"). The recordings are CC0 or CC BY, with their credits in `assets/CREDITS.md`.
- **R8.** A parked scooter within 10 m of each start point of `data/geo/starts.json`, so that a test can ride at once. The URL parameter `ride` of the web build (and the argument `--ride`) starts the game on that scooter.
- **R9.** The world streams in time for a ride at 40 km/h: the ground, the town, the houses of the streets, the trees, and the showcase are ready before the player sees them. PT-80 gives the longest frame and the count of frames longer than three times the median frame, along the ride. I set the limit after the first measure.
- **R10.** `PlaytestDriver.ride_along(path, speed)`: a play test rides a route with the joystick (real input), as `stick_along()` walks one. An agent that tests the town uses it to go far fast.
- **R11.** AGENTS.md, section 5: the fixed decision "View and controls" with the scooter and the joystick at its rest place, and the commands.

## 8. Builder notes (2026-11-23)

- **The clock (R1, R2).** `GameState.advance()` moves the clock by the time of the frames (the game time, with `Engine.time_scale`), with the seconds, which the save keeps (`seconds` in place of `walk_m`; a save of version 2 without it starts the minute at 0 s). It runs from the first frame, and in the Vũng Tàu world from the frame where the ground of the start is ready (at most 60 s after, `Main.CLOCK_WAIT_S`). The play tests that counted on the old rule, and their changes: PT-11 `fp_walk` (the clock moves by the time of the frames, not by the distance); PT-05 `save_reload` (after the restart the same minute or the next one: the clock runs before the quit and after the start); the soak (the run of the cart of mía moves the clock one minute from now, not to the opening minute plus one, which the clock can be at already); PT-77 `showcase_steps` (the set of the walkers that show comes from the minute: the run holds the clock at its time with `PlaytestDriver.hold_clock()`, as before; a walker stood in opening 4 when the clock ran). - **The motion (D2, D4, D5).** `ScooterMotion` runs in each physics step; `tests/unit/test_scooter.gd` checks the same path at 3 and at 60 frames each second, and PT-80 checks 0 to 20 km/h (4 s) and the brake (4 m/s²) in physics steps. ### The corrections of report 0018 (2026-11-24)

For each defect, the play-test step came first and failed; then the correction.

- **D4 and R5 (changed).** `ScooterMotion.max_steer_at()`: at most 35°, and at most the turn that gives 3.5 m/s² to the side (`SIDE_ACCELERATION`): 29.7° at 10 km/h, 8.1° at 20 km/h, 3.6° at 30 km/h, 2.0° at 40 km/h. The turn of the handlebars also stays in this limit while the speed goes up. `tests/unit/test_scooter.gd` checks the full turn to each side at 5, 10, 20, 30, and 40 km/h, and a held turn while the scooter speeds up. `ride_along()` already slowed for a bend to 3 m/s² to the side; it now takes the limit of the turn from `max_steer_at()`. PT-80 run 1 rides at the top speed to 60 m before the junction at Cầu Đá (not 25 m): then the rider slows to 20 km/h for the corner.
- **D-0018-01: the curb at a low speed.** PT-80 run 3: up the curb of the sidewalk of the park on Quang Trung (x 99.5 m, z 60 m, to the west) at 3 km/h, then to the north along the sidewalk, and up the same curb at a small angle at 20 km/h; after each, at least 5 m on the sidewalk with no bump (the floor under the middle of the scooter is the showcase, not the ground of the road), and a screenshot on the curb with the grips in the view (`PlaytestDriver.grips_in_view()`). The step found three causes. (1) At 3 km/h the step of one physics frame is about 1.4 cm: after the step up, the round front of the capsule was still over the edge, and the snap put it on the corner again (the height went between +0.01 m and +0.25 m, as in the report). Now, after a step up, the scooter keeps its height (no gravity, no snap) until the floor under the round end in front is near its height (the top of the curb), for at most 1.2 m (`Player._climb`). (2) At a small angle the scooter slid along the curb for 20 m: a contact at less than 30° was a slide, and the step was tried only for a hit. Now each contact with a step tries it. (3) On the curb the scooter tilts about 9°: the model turned about its base, the view about the eye, and the grips went under the view. Now the model turns about the eye, as one body with the rider. A step comes into the view in 0.15 s, not in one frame. `start_at()` on the scooter (`Player.place()`) keeps the pitch of the seat.
- **D-0018-02: "Get off" in the middle of the view.** PT-81 checks at each size and at each step on the scooter that the button is not in the middle third of the view (I read it as the box of the middle third of the width and of the height, around the center dot: the road ahead), and that it does not cover the subtitles or the phone button; it taps "Get off" at a speed (the player stays on, and "Slow down to get off." shows). On the scooter the button is at the right, above the phone button, at each speed, so that it does not move under the thumb when the scooter slows (`Main._place_action_button()`). The subtitles keep out of its place. Off the scooter the action button stays under the center dot.
- **D-0018-03: `--ride` before the showcase is built.** PT-80 run 2 takes its first screenshot at the first frame of the run, before any input, then pushes the throttle for 1 s. The showcase near the start was not built, and the scooter rode 1.4 m. Now a scooter that stands does not start while `VungTau.showcase_pending()` is true (`Player.ride_hold`; a scooter that moves goes on), and a push shows "The street is not ready yet. Wait a moment." (`data/ui.json`, `scooter_wait`). The world does not build the showcase before the first frame: the first frame comes as early as before.
- **D-0018-04: the paths of a ride.** PT-80 run 4: "Get off" at more than 2 km/h; a ride through an opening of the low wall of the beach with no steps (the sand near the height of the walk), one through an opening with steps down to the sand, and one from the road next to Hồ Mây Thượng (Núi Lớn) to the middle of the lake.
