# Culture notes

This file lists what we know about the food and customs in the game.

Status values:

- **Confirmed**: I know it first-hand. It is correct for Vũng Tàu and the South.
- **Source**: a published source says it. I did not confirm it yet.
- **Check**: not sure. Do not use it in the game without my approval.

## Phở

| Fact | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| In Vietnam, phở is breakfast. Abroad, people eat it for lunch or dinner. | Confirmed | First-hand; [Hoc24](https://hoc24.vn/cau-hoi/viet-mot-doan-van-noi-ve-mon-an-nuoc-uong-ma-minh-yeu-thich-bang-tieng-anh-goi-y-mon-an-ma-ban-yeu-thich-la-gi-no-co-vi-nhu-the-nao.171435468365) |
| Many phở shops sell out by late morning. Some shops open from 5:00 to 11:00. | Source | [Foody: Phở Dậu](https://www.foody.vn/ho-chi-minh/pho-dau-nam-ky-khoi-nghia) |
| People order with the cuts in one phrase: "một tô phở tái nạm gầu bò viên". They do not only say "phở tái". | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Cuts: tái (thin raw beef, cooks in the broth), nạm (flank), gầu (fatty brisket), gân (tendon), sách (tripe), bò viên (meatballs). "Đặc biệt" means all cuts. | Source | [Seattle Times pho guide](https://projects.seattletimes.com/2016/food/pho-guide) |
| "Nước béo hành trần": a small bowl of fat from the top of the pot, with scallion. People put the onion in the bowl. They mix hoisin and chili sauce into the fat and dip the meat. You must ask for it. | Confirmed (name), Source (use) | First-hand; [Seattle Times pho guide](https://projects.seattletimes.com/2016/food/pho-guide) |
| "Chén trứng": an egg in its own small bowl. You must ask for it. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| How people eat the chén trứng (broth over the egg, or into the bowl). | Check | None |
| The herb plate (húng quế, ngò gai, giá, chanh, ớt) is part of the dish. Many visitors abroad do not use it. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Many people put hoisin and chili sauce in a small dish to dip the meat. They keep the broth clean. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Phở came South with Northern families after 1954. In the South, the broth became richer and the herb plate became bigger. | Source | [Vietcetera](https://vietcetera.com/vn/pho-goi-ghem-muon-vi-nhan-gian-trong-mot-bat-an) |
| Phở Bắc: clear broth, wide noodles, few herbs. Some Northern people do not want hoisin or sriracha in phở. Some Northern shops do not have bean sprouts or hoisin. | Source | [Vietnam Tourism](https://www.vietnamtourism.com/vi/bac-vs-trung-vs-nam-am-thuc-viet-nam-thay-oi-the-nao-tren-kh); [Foody review](https://www.foody.vn/ho-chi-minh/pho-dau-nam-ky-khoi-nghia) |
| You pay after you eat. You call "Cô ơi, tính tiền!". | Check | Prototype text |
| Tips are not expected at a quán. | Check | Prototype text |
| Prices: a bowl costs about 50.000₫ to 60.000₫. | Check | Placeholder |

## Coffee

| Fact | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| People order "một ly đen đá", "một ly nâu đá", or "bạc sỉu". | Confirmed | First-hand |
| "Nâu đá" is the word in Hà Nội. In the South, people say "sữa đá". Which word do people use in Vũng Tàu? | Check | [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/muon-ve-ca-phe-o-sai-gon-2725708.html) |
| Bạc sỉu: coffee with fresh milk and condensed milk, more milk than coffee. It is a Southern name. | Source | [Exotic Voyages](https://www.exoticvoyages.com/blog/phin-filter-in-vietnamese-coffee-culture-35848.html) |
| Coffee drips through a phin. Robusta beans are about 95% of the coffee from Vietnam. | Source | [Vietnam Airlines](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/en/useful-information/travel-guide/vietnamese-drip-coffee) |
| Cà phê cóc (cà phê ghế đẩu): small plastic stools, a wall ledge as a table. Office workers and students sit side by side. | Source | [Báo Quốc tế](https://baoquocte.vn/muon-kieu-cafe-sai-gon-6155.html) |

## Vũng Tàu

| Fact | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| In the early morning, many people exercise at Bãi Trước. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Fishing boats come in early. People buy fresh fish from the boats. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Before sunrise, the catch is sorted on the sand at the Bến Đình fishing village. | Source | [Vietnam Travel](https://vietnam.travel/jp/node/745) |
| Bánh khọt: small crisp cakes of rice flour with curry powder, topped with shrimp or squid. People wrap them in lettuce and herbs and dip them in a fish sauce mix. | Source | [Vietnam Travel](https://vietnam.travel/jp/node/745) |
| In the evening, people drive scooters along the coast road. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Helmets are required on motorbikes on all roads since December 2007. | Check | Not verified |
| People walk to Miếu Hòn Bà when the tide is low. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| Hòn Bà is an islet in the Bãi Sau area, about 200 m from Mũi Nghinh Phong and the foot of Núi Nhỏ. A rock path of a few hundred meters shows only at low tide. It is slippery, with pebbles and oyster shells. Boats go to the east side of the islet at high tide. | Source | [Báo Lào Cai](https://baolaocai.vn/hon-dao-co-duong-di-luc-an-luc-hien-o-vung-tau-khach-ghe-tham-phai-canh-ngay-post389986.html) |
| The path shows for a few hours on a few days each month. The sea goes down most on the 14th to the 16th of the lunar month. Visitors start from the Thùy Vân slope, walk about 10 minutes on the sand, then 15 to 20 minutes to the islet. | Source | [Báo Lào Cai](https://baolaocai.vn/hon-dao-co-duong-di-luc-an-luc-hien-o-vung-tau-khach-ghe-tham-phai-canh-ngay-post389986.html); [VinWonders](https://vinwonders.com/vi/wonderpedia/news/hon-ba-vung-tau/) |
| Miếu Hòn Bà honors the Water Dragon Goddess (Thủy Long Thần nữ), who helps fishermen. People gather there on the 15th of the 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th lunar months. | Source | [Báo Lào Cai](https://baolaocai.vn/hon-dao-co-duong-di-luc-an-luc-hien-o-vung-tau-khach-ghe-tham-phai-canh-ngay-post389986.html) |
| The tide at Vũng Tàu is mixed, mainly semi-diurnal. The mean range is about 2.5 m. | Source | [Vimaru JMST](https://jmst.vimaru.edu.vn/index.php/jmst/article/download/891/646/1911) |
| Many people jog or ride scooters up the Vi Ba road (núi Viba) in the middle and the end of the afternoon. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| At the end of the Vi Ba road, a few small shops sell yogurt (sữa chua) and soft-boiled eggs (trứng lòng đào). | Confirmed | First-hand |
| In the middle of the Vi Ba road is a pagoda with many monkeys. People stop to watch them. | Confirmed | First-hand |
| The pagoda with the monkeys is Chùa Tam Bảo (chùa Khỉ), 72 Vi Ba. | Confirmed | First-hand; [Foody](https://www.foody.vn/vung-tau/chua-tam-bao); [VinWonders](https://vinwonders.com/vi/wonderpedia/news/nui-lon-vung-tau/) |
| Wild monkeys on Núi Lớn sometimes go into houses and bite people. | Source | [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/nui-lon.html) |
| Town anchors that have been in the same place for a long time: the main bus station, the old markets, the big roundabout, and Ngã Năm. | Confirmed | First-hand. I will name the markets and the roundabout. |

## Daily life

| Fact | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Ăn sáng chưa?" (Have you eaten breakfast yet?) is a morning greeting. | Check | Prototype text |

## Games for reference

- **Venba** (Visai Games): a cooking game about Tamil food. The recipes are puzzles. The player learns by trial and error, with a gentle reaction to mistakes. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venba_(video_game))
- **Brother Hai's Pho Restaurant**: a Vietnamese indie game made with Godot. Players liked its true details of daily life, such as short stools and floor tiles. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brother_Hai%27s_Pho_Restaurant)
