## Tượng Chúa Kitô Vua (Núi Nhỏ)

Photos:

- [Núi Nhỏ Vũng Tàu: view ngắm phố biển, tượng Chúa Kito (VinWonders)](https://vinwonders.com/vi/wonderpedia/news/tuong-dai-chua-kito-vung-tau/): aerial view from the front, with the base.

- "Towering statue of Jesus Christ in Vung Tau" (Vietnamnet, Báo ảnh Việt Nam): the upper body from the front, at the height of the chest.
- "13 Vung Tau tourism destinations…" (Lily's Travel Agency): from above and in front, with the plan of the base.
- "Tên gọi vùng đất Vũng Tàu" (PN-Hiệp, blogspot): aerial view from the front.
- [Category:Christ of Vung Tau (Wikimedia Commons)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Christ_of_Vung_Tau).

Sizes from the sources: the figure is 32 m from the feet to the top of the halo, and the arms are 18.4 m from hand to hand ([Wikipedia, vi](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C6%B0%E1%BB%A3ng_Ch%C3%BAa_Kit%C3%B4_Vua_(V%C5%A9ng_T%C3%A0u))). 133 steps go up inside to the shoulders (Wikipedia, vi; I confirm the climb).

Sizes measured from the photos, on 2026-11-06. The scale is the arm span (18.4 m) at the depth of the figure, and the height (32 m) for the vertical sizes. The photos are from above and at an angle, so a size from them is correct to about 15 %. A size of the base is less exact than a size of the figure, because the base is at another distance from the camera.
