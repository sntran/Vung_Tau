# Houses: reference photos

This page lists each part of the house detail kit (plan 0004, R7) with links to public photos on the web, for comparison with the look review (PT-25). These are links only. The repository has no copies of the photos.

The links go to public pages: Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons (search pages and categories), and news articles. Most photos are of Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City, not Vũng Tàu, so check each part against Vũng Tàu.

In the last column, I write "correct" or "not correct" for Vũng Tàu, with a note. "Not checked" means that I did not look yet.
